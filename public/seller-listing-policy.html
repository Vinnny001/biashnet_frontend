Seller listing policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Every product on Biashnet is checked by our team before buyers can see it. This policy explains what you can sell, and what your photos, titles and descriptions must follow, so your listings are approved first time.

In short
  • Sell only real, legal items that you have now and can bring to Biashnet within 36 hours of a sale.
  • Use clear photos of the actual item, and describe it honestly.
  • No phone numbers, links or social media handles, and never ask buyers to pay you directly.
  • Changing a listing's photos or text sends it back for review. Changing price or stock doesn't.

1. How listings are reviewed

  • New listings are reviewed before they appear to buyers.
  • Editing a listing's photos, title, name, description, category, subcategory, condition or location sends it back for review. It's hidden from buyers until it's approved again, because the changes must meet this policy too.
  • Changing the price or stock doesn't need review, so your listing stays live.
  • Deleting a listing doesn't need review and takes effect straight away.
  • If a listing is rejected, fix what doesn't meet this policy and save it. It goes back for review.

2. Items you can't sell

These can't be listed on Biashnet under any circumstances:

  • Illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia
  • Prescription-only medicines
  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapons
  • Fireworks and hazardous, flammable or toxic substances
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes and e-cigarettes
  • Stolen goods
  • Counterfeit or fake branded goods
  • Pirated films, music, games or software
  • Live animals
  • Wildlife products, such as ivory or animal skins
  • Plastic carrier bags banned in Kenya
  • Adult or sexual products and content
  • ID cards, passports, licences and other official documents
  • Registered SIM cards, M-PESA or bank accounts
  • Hacked accounts, passwords or personal data
  • Recalled or unsafe products
  • Anything promoting hatred, violence or discrimination
  • Anything else that is illegal to sell in Kenya

Services, digital downloads and gift cards also can't be listed. Biashnet is for physical items delivered through our store.

3. Photos

Do

  • Use your own photos of the actual item you'll deliver
  • Show the whole item, in focus and well lit
  • Make the first photo the main item. It's your cover image
  • Show any damage, marks or wear on used items
  • Add photos from different angles

Don't

  • Put phone numbers, WhatsApp, social handles, links or QR codes in photos
  • Use photos taken from other sellers or websites
  • Use watermarks or logos from other shops
  • Show people who haven't agreed to appear
  • Include nudity, violence or offensive content
  • Upload photos unrelated to the item

Manufacturer images may be used only for new, sealed items identical to what the buyer will receive.

4. Titles and descriptions

Do

  • Say exactly what the item is: brand, model, size, colour and quantity
  • Choose the right category and condition
  • Describe faults, missing parts or wear honestly
  • Write in English or Swahili

Don't

  • Include phone numbers, emails, links or social media handles
  • Call an item "original", "genuine" or "new" if it isn't
  • Write in ALL CAPS, or repeat keywords or emoji to game search
  • Use offensive, discriminatory or misleading language
  • List several different items in one listing

5. Price and stock

  • Set a real price in Kenyan shillings for the item as described.
  • Only show an old ("was") price if you genuinely sold it at that price. No invented discounts.
  • Don't set an unrealistically low price just to attract buyers.
  • List only stock you have now, and keep the stock count accurate.

6. Payments and delivery

  • Buyers pay through Biashnet's M-PESA checkout, and we hold the payment until the order is delivered. Never ask a buyer to pay you directly: that payment isn't protected and breaks this policy.
  • When an order is paid, you'll be notified which of your items to bring to the Biashnet store and by when. You have 36 hours.
  • If you miss the drop-off window, the buyer can cancel or accept the rest of the order, and you won't be paid for the items you didn't deliver.
  • Biashnet delivers to the buyer. You don't need, and won't be given, the buyer's contact details.
  • Your earnings are released to your seller wallet once the buyer confirms delivery, and you can withdraw them to M-PESA.

7. Brands and other people's content

  • Only use a brand name when the item genuinely is that brand.
  • Don't copy other sellers' photos or descriptions.
  • If you believe a listing uses your content or brand without permission, contact us using the details below.

8. If a listing breaks these rules

  • The listing is rejected or removed.
  • Orders for prohibited items may be cancelled and buyers refunded.
  • Repeated or serious breaches can lead to your seller account being suspended.
  • Where the law requires, we report illegal items to the relevant authorities.

9. Questions and reporting

If you're not sure whether an item is allowed, or want to report a listing, email biashnetltd@gmail.com.
